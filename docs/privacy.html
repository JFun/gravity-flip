Privacy Policy

Gravity Flip · Last updated 2026-05-05

This privacy policy explains what data the Gravity Flip mobile app collects and how it is used. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

The app uses Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to understand how players use the game so we can improve it. Firebase Analytics automatically collects:

  • App usage events — for example, when a level starts, when a level is completed, when the player dies. We do not log any text, voice, or content you create.
  • Device identifiers — a Firebase Installation ID and Apple's IDFV (Identifier for Vendor). These are anonymous identifiers tied to the app on your device, not to you personally.
  • Device information — model (e.g., "iPhone 13 Pro"), iOS version, screen orientation, and app version.
  • Coarse location — country and region, derived from IP address. We do not collect precise GPS location.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or other personal identifying information.
  • No precise location, GPS, or address.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No payment information (the app is free and contains no purchases).

How the data is used

Aggregated analytics data helps us answer questions like "how far do players get before they stop?" and "which levels are too hard?" so we can improve the game. The data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Data retention

Firebase Analytics retains event data for up to 14 months by default, after which it is automatically deleted by Google. Aggregated, anonymized reports may be retained longer.

Third parties

Firebase Analytics is operated by Google. Google's privacy policy applies to data they process on our behalf: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not share data with any other third parties.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The data Firebase Analytics collects (anonymous identifiers, device type, app interactions) is the same for all users regardless of age.

Your choices

You can opt out of Firebase Analytics collection by enabling "Limit Ad Tracking" in your iOS settings, or by deleting the app from your device. Reinstalling generates a new anonymous ID with no link to the previous one.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@example.com.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date at the top and post a note on the support page.
